# The Tired Dark Lord

A 100-word villain monologue for the make.fun bounty "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue". A dark lord who built an empire of obsidian and bone — seven kingdoms, nine cursed banners — greets the latest farm-boy hero, and his tiredness lives in the pauses, the loose armrest of the skull throne, and one quiet evening he will never get. The program writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`.
